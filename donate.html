About Internet Privacy Foundation

Internet Privacy Foundation (IPF) defends digital freedom and the fundamental right to privacy. Through open-source projects including Marmot and White Noise, IPF develops protocols, libraries, and applications that help people communicate privately and give developers the tools to build on that work.

IPF is a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Donations are tax deductible for U.S. taxpayers to the extent permitted by law.

What your support helps make possible

Open-source infrastructure needs ongoing work: implementing features, reviewing changes, testing how clients work together, and helping people build on it. Donations help IPF continue that work.

Protocol and libraries

Developing the specification and the implementations that turn it into working software.

Testing and review

Checking interoperability, reviewing changes, and investigating problems across clients.

Tools and documentation

Making it easier for developers to understand Marmot, integrate it, and contribute improvements.

Continued maintenance

Supporting the ongoing work behind Marmot, White Noise, and private communication software.

About your donation

Donations are received by Internet Privacy Foundation and support its general work, including Marmot and White Noise. They are not restricted to Marmot alone.

IPF and its payment providers handle payment details and confirmations.
